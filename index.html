Secular Homeschooling, Organized Together

FAHA provides the organization, group funding, and trip scholarships so families can lead clubs, build community, and make homeschool memorable.

Join Our Discord

Member-Run. Community-Backed.

FAHA isn’t a top-down school—it’s an engine built to empower homeschool families to start clubs, plan field trips, and support one another.

Member-Driven Clubs

Parents and student leaders initiate the activities they want to see—from science clubs and robotics to outdoor hikes and art meetups.

Organizational Funds

FAHA holds the non-profit infrastructure, insurance, and funds needed to help members buy club supplies, secure venues, and organize larger events.

Trip Scholarships

We believe cost should never prevent a child from participating. FAHA raises funds to provide need-based trip scholarships for member families.

Financial Accessibility

Homeschool Trip Scholarships

Every year, FAHA organizes group trips, museum visits, and educational camps. Through community donations, we maintain a dedicated scholarship fund so every registered family can apply for partial or full trip assistance.
