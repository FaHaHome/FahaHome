A Member-Organized Secular Homeschool Community

FAHA provides the non-profit framework, club funding, and trip scholarships so families can organize inclusive activities together.

Join Membership Discord Hub

Member-Led, Community-Supported

We aren't a traditional school. FAHA is a non-profit engine that equips families to start clubs, plan outings, and build community.

Member-Driven Clubs

Parents and students start and lead the clubs they want—from robotics and science to nature hikes and gaming groups.

Organizational Funds

FAHA holds the non-profit status, insurance, and budget needed to fund member club supplies and venue rentals.

Inclusive & Secular

Strictly secular educational spaces where all homeschooling families are welcomed, respected, and supported.

Financial Accessibility

Homeschool Trip Scholarships

Cost should never keep a child from learning. FAHA raises non-profit funds to provide need-based trip scholarships for member families attending group outings and educational events.
